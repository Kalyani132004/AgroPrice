About AgroPrice

AgroPrice is an agritech platform built to close the information gap between farmers and mandi markets. We aggregate daily mandi prices, historical trends, and profit analytics into one clean, accessible dashboard — so farmers can decide where, when, and at what price to sell their produce.

Our mission is simple: give every farmer the same market intelligence that large traders already have.
